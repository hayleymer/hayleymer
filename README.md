## Featured projects

### [Homelessness services in Australia](https://github.com/hayleymer/homelessness-australia-analysis)

Nine years of Australian Institute of Health and Welfare data on specialist homelessness services. The headline finding is a warning against trusting the headline metric: the number of people served each year is essentially flat (+0.1% since 2017-18), while requests that go unassisted have risen roughly 40%. A system that looks steady on its most-watched number is in fact turning more people away.

`Python` · `pandas` · `SQL` · `Tableau`

### [Melbourne metropolitan train timetable and demand](https://github.com/hayleymer/melbourne-train-analysis)

GTFS schedule data and Transport Victoria patronage statistics, used to ask whether service supply matches where passengers actually travel. Stony Point carries the network's largest service gap, CBD stations show a heavy evening-peak skew, and overall patronage has recovered to 75.8% of its pre-COVID baseline: a gap with direct implications for any timetable review.

`Python` · `SQLite / SQL` · `pandas` · `Tableau`
